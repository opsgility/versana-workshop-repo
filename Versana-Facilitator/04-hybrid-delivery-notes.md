# Hybrid Delivery Notes — Part 1 Virtual + Part 2 Onsite

Versana indicated a possible hybrid model: a virtual overview (Part 1) followed by an onsite deeper dive a few weeks later (Part 2). This file covers how to split the content between the two sessions and what to ask attendees to do between them.

> **If you're running a single 1+1 hour session instead of the hybrid:** ignore this file. Use `00-facilitator-guide.md`, `01-hour1-overview-outline.md`, and `02-hour2-use-case-library.md` as written.

---

## When the hybrid model fits

Run the hybrid when at least two of these are true:

- The audience is large enough that hour 2's live Q&A would be too crowded to be useful (>30 people)
- Most of the audience is brand new to Copilot — they need time to use it before they know what to ask
- Onsite delivery is logistically possible 2–4 weeks after Part 1
- You want broader cross-team exposure (mixed roles in Part 1, then deeper role-specific work in Part 2)

If the audience is smaller, more experienced, or ready to dig in, a single 1+1 hour session is faster and simpler.

---

## Part 1 — Virtual overview (1 hour, max 90 min)

**Goal:** Get every attendee to write and use one Copilot prompt by end of session, and to leave with a homework assignment.

**Use:** `01-hour1-overview-outline.md` — but compress the live Q&A portion. Save deep questions for Part 2.

**What changes from the standalone format:**

| Block | Standalone (1+1 hour) | Hybrid Part 1 (virtual) |
|---|---|---|
| Welcome + framework | 15 min | 15 min — same |
| Quick-win demos | 40 min | 30 min — drop one demo (typically Excel) |
| Live Q&A | 60 min (hour 2) | 10 min — only enough to surface topics for Part 2 |
| **Total** | 120 min | **55 min** |

**End of Part 1 — give homework.** This is the critical move that makes Part 2 work.

> *"Between now and Part 2 in [date], pick one of these three things to do:*
> *(1) Write a real status email or memo using the prompt sheet I just shared.*
> *(2) Use Copilot to summarize a meeting recording or a long Teams thread you've been ignoring.*
> *(3) Try to use Copilot for something you'd normally do manually, and bring it to Part 2 — whether it worked or didn't.*
> *Bring questions, friction points, and 'I tried this and it didn't work' moments. Part 2 is for the things you actually run into."*

---

## Between the two sessions (2–4 weeks)

**What to do:**
- Post a 1-line check-in in the Teams channel halfway through ("How's it going? Drop a question in the chat — I'll pull good ones into Part 2")
- Capture every question that comes in
- Share the use-case library link in the Teams channel; let people self-serve between sessions
- Optional: schedule one 30-minute drop-in office hour the week before Part 2 for anyone who wants 1:1 help getting unstuck

**What attendees should do:**
- Use Copilot at least 3–5 times in real work
- Note which prompts worked and which didn't
- Bring at least one specific question to Part 2

## Part 2 — Onsite deeper dive (2 hours)

**Goal:** Solve real problems together. Build muscle in the apps that matter most for each role.

**Format:** ~30 min facilitator-led, ~90 min hands-on / live work / small-group exercises.

### Suggested agenda

| Time | Block | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 – 0:15 | Recap + check-in | "What worked? What didn't? What did you try?" Take 3–4 stories from the room. |
| 0:15 – 0:30 | Patterns from Part 1 | Show 2–3 of the most common questions from the homework period. Demo solutions live. Pull from the use-case library. |
| 0:30 – 1:15 | Hands-on rotations (45 min) | Split into 2 or 3 small groups by role lean (ops / product / mixed). Each group works through 2–3 use cases from the library on their own work. Facilitator floats between groups. |
| 1:15 – 1:30 | Group share-back | Each group reports one prompt that worked and one that didn't. Facilitator demos a fix for the latter. |
| 1:30 – 1:55 | Open Q&A and live work | Like hour 2 in the standalone format — but now the room knows what to ask, because they've used Copilot. |
| 1:55 – 2:00 | Habits and close | Reinforce the three habits: framework, iterate, SharePoint. Confirm any follow-up office hours. |

### Hands-on rotation suggestions

Pick 2–3 of these for the 45-minute hands-on block, depending on the room:

| Rotation | Apps | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Status & summary** | Outlook + Teams | Universal — everyone has emails and meetings |
| **Memo & deck** | Word + PowerPoint | Product, ops, leadership |
| **Data spotter** | Excel | Ops, finance, procurement |
| **Inbox cleanup** | Outlook | Anyone behind on email |
| **Channel catchup** | Teams | Cross-functional roles |

Run each rotation as ~15 minutes of guided practice on a real piece of attendees' own work — not a fake exercise. The use-case library has the prompts; attendees substitute their own context.

## Recommended delivery cadence (if running multiple cohorts)

If Versana ends up running multiple cohorts of this content (e.g., 110-person champion group, executive assistants, operations team, product team):

| Cohort | Format suggestion |
|---|---|
| Champion group / first wave (15–25) | Single 1+1 hour session (no hybrid) — they'll be your evangelists |
| Broad ops + product (large cohorts) | Hybrid — virtual Part 1 for everyone, onsite Part 2 in smaller groups |
| Exec assistants | Single 1+1 hour session, role-tuned to drafting and meeting prep |
| Developers (already on GitHub Copilot) | 30-min "M365 Copilot for the rest of your day" session — focused on Outlook, Teams, Word; skip the framework lecture |
| Executives | 30-min focused demo session — what their EAs and teams will be doing, plus 2–3 prompts they'd use personally |

## What success looks like

- Each attendee has used Copilot in real work between Part 1 and Part 2
- Part 2 covers questions and friction points the audience surfaced — not a re-lecture
- The Teams channel between sessions has at least 5–10 questions or shared experiences
- After Part 2, at least one person per role volunteers to be a champion / power user for their function

If those four things happen, the hybrid was worth running. If they don't, the next cohort is probably better off with the single 1+1 hour format.
